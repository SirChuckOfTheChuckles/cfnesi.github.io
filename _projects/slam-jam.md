**Slam Jam!** is a team-developed 2D puzzle-platformer released on itch.io for Windows and macOS. Players run, jump, and use a ground-slam mechanic to interact with instruments and follow golden musical notes through the level.

### Highlights

- Contributed as a developer on a released small-team game project.
- Helped ship a downloadable game build for both Windows and macOS.
- Worked on a project centered around readable game mechanics, player movement, and puzzle-platformer interaction design.

### Notable Contributions

<div class="contribution-list">
  <article>
    <h3>Expanded player movement with sprinting</h3>
    <p>Movement initially felt too constrained for the kind of puzzle-platforming freedom the team wanted.</p>
    <p>I helped add a sprint button to the character, which made traversal feel more responsive and opened up more expressive map design.</p>
    <p>The change required reworking some levels, but it gave the project much greater freedom in how spaces, jumps, and timing challenges could be built.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h3>Built timed obstacle sections around quick reactions</h3>
    <p>Some map obstacles felt too basic when used on their own, even though the core mechanics were readable and fun.</p>
    <p>I helped create timed map sections that pushed players to think quickly, react under pressure, and use familiar mechanics in more demanding ways.</p>
    <p>That turned simple interactions into challenges that felt fresh, more energetic, and more fun to replay.</p>
  </article>
</div>
